About RetinaAI

A multimodal deep learning system for automated Diabetic Retinopathy staging using dual-stream image analysis and gradient-boosted classification.

Pipeline
Model Architecture
Step 01
Image Input
Fundus photo + OCT scan resized to 224×224 px and normalised to [0, 1].
Step 02
Feature Extraction
DenseNet121 (fundus → 1024-d) and DenseNet121 (OCT → 1024-d) via Global Average Pooling.
Step 03
Feature Fusion
Vectors concatenated to 2048-d and passed through a fitted StandardScaler.
Step 04
Classification
LightGBM (500 estimators, lr 0.05) outputs the DR stage and confidence probability.
